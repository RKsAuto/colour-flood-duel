Tap a colour, press 1–6, or tap any tile on the board. The number under each colour is how many cells it grabs right now.

How to play

  • Each player owns the patch connected to their corner.
  • On your turn, pick a new colour. Your whole patch turns that colour and absorbs every touching cell of the same colour.
  • You can't pick your own colour or your rival's. Your pick also locks that colour away from them next turn.
  • Own more than half the board to win. If nobody captures a cell for 3 turns each, the bigger territory wins.
  • The first move alternates every round.
