Tap a colour, press 1–6, or tap any tile on the board. The number under each colour is how many tiles it grabs right now, and ★ means it sets off a power tile.

How to play

  • Each player owns the patch connected to their corner. Dark gaps are walls and aren't part of the board.
  • On your turn, pick a new colour. Your whole patch turns that colour and absorbs every touching tile of the same colour.
  • You can't pick your own colour or your rival's. Your pick also locks that colour away from them next turn.
  • Burst tiles blast open the 8 tiles around them, whatever their colour.
  • Prism tiles match every colour, so any flood that touches one takes it.
  • Raid tiles steal every rival tile touching your border. Home corners can't be stolen.
  • Own more than half the playable tiles to win. If nobody captures a tile for 3 turns each, the bigger territory wins.
  • The first move alternates every round.
